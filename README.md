## Why Learn the Stock Market?

Understanding the stock market involves more than buying and selling shares. It requires knowledge of market fundamentals, price movements, company performance, and risk management.

A structured learning programme can help you understand:

- **Stock Market Basics:** Learn how the NSE, BSE, shares, and trading accounts work.
- **Technical Analysis:** Understand candlestick patterns, support and resistance, and price charts.
- **Fundamental Analysis:** Learn how to evaluate companies using financial statements and important ratios.
- **IPO Analysis:** Understand IPOs, issue details, and the factors investors consider before applying.
- **Risk Management:** Learn about stop-loss orders, position sizing, and the importance of controlling risk.
